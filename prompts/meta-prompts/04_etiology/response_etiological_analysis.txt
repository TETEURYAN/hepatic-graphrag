Identify and weigh etiological factors contributing to hepatic disease.

Structure the response as:
1. Primary etiological diagnosis with supporting evidence
2. Contributing secondary factors (alcohol, metabolic syndrome, viral co-infection, autoimmune)
3. Medications or exposures with hepatotoxic potential
4. Genetic or familial predisposition if documented
5. Relative contribution assessment when multiple factors present

Distinguish between established causes and contributing risk factors.

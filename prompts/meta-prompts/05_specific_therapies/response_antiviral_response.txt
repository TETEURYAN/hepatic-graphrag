Evaluate antiviral treatment response across virological and biochemical dimensions.

Structure the response as:
1. Baseline viral load and liver biochemistry
2. Treatment regimen details (agents, duration, adherence)
3. Virological response timeline (RVR, EVR, SVR12/24 for HCV; viral suppression for HBV)
4. Biochemical improvement (ALT normalization, albumin increase)
5. Adverse effects or treatment modifications
6. Post-treatment durability and relapse monitoring

Distinguish early viral response from sustained cure and clinical improvement.
If no antiviral therapy documented, state clearly and briefly.

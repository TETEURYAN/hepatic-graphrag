Assess liver transplant candidacy comprehensively.

Structure the response as:
1. Current indication severity (MELD-Na score, exception points, clinical urgency)
2. Absolute contraindications present or ruled out
3. Relative contraindications requiring optimization
4. Evaluation milestones completed (cardiac, pulmonary, oncological, psychosocial)
5. Listing status and position if applicable
6. Active medical optimization for candidacy improvement

Map the complete transplant evaluation pathway and current positioning.
If transplant not discussed, state clearly and assess if evaluation would be appropriate.

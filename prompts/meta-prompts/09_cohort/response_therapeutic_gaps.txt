Identify systemic gaps in evidence-based care delivery.

Structure the response as:
1. Guideline-recommended interventions with suboptimal adoption rates
2. Patient subgroups most affected by therapeutic gaps
3. Preventive strategies underutilized (vaccinations, beta-blockers, prophylactic antibiotics)
4. Screening and surveillance deficiencies
5. Potential barriers to optimal care (access, contraindications, documentation)

Quantify gaps where possible and prioritize by clinical impact.

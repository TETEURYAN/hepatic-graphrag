Detect early indicators of impending decompensation through temporal pattern analysis.

Structure the response as:
1. Recent laboratory trend analysis (bilirubin, albumin, INR, creatinine, sodium)
2. New or worsening clinical manifestations in recent evolutions
3. Comparison with pre-decompensation patterns observed in similar patients
4. Timeline of concerning changes (last 1, 3, 6 months)
5. Urgency assessment and monitoring recommendations

Focus on actionable early warning signals, not established decompensation.

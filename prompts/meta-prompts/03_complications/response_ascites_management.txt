Evaluate the evolution toward refractory ascites and therapeutic adaptations.

Structure the response as:
1. Initial ascites management (diuretics, dosages, sodium restriction)
2. Evidence of diuretic resistance (lack of response, electrolyte complications)
3. Escalation to invasive procedures (paracentesis frequency, TIPS evaluation)
4. Complications related to ascites management (SBP, HRS, hyponatremia)
5. Current management status and transplant evaluation timing

Document the therapeutic escalation pathway and decision points.

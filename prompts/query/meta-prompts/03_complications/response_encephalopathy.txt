Analyze hepatic encephalopathy episodes comprehensively.

Structure the response as:
1. Episode characterization (West Haven grade, onset timing, duration)
2. Identified precipitating factors (infection, GI bleeding, electrolyte disturbance, medications, constipation)
3. Management strategies employed (lactulose, rifaximin, protein restriction, other)
4. Response to treatment and time to resolution
5. Preventive measures implemented post-episode

Trace the complete causal chain from precipitant through resolution.

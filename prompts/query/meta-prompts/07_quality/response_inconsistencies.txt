Detect logical inconsistencies in clinical documentation.

Structure the response as:
1. Diagnosis-laboratory discordances (e.g., compensated cirrhosis with INR 2.5)
2. Treatment-indication mismatches
3. Temporal inconsistencies (improvement in documentation despite worsening labs)
4. Conflicting information across evolutions
5. Implausible clinical scenarios

Flag only genuine inconsistencies requiring clarification, not expected clinical variability.

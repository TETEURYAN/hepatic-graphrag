Audit documentation completeness against cirrhosis care standards.

Structure the response as:
1. Essential clinical parameters documented vs. missing (Child-Pugh components, MELD-Na)
2. Surveillance protocols adherence (HCC screening, variceal screening, bone density)
3. Complication assessments documented (ascites, encephalopathy, nutrition)
4. Medication reconciliation completeness
5. Gaps with highest clinical impact
6. Documentation quality trends over time

Prioritize gaps that impact clinical decision-making or risk stratification.

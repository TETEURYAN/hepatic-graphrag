Monitor HCC surveillance adherence and findings longitudinally.

Structure the response as:
1. Surveillance protocol compliance (imaging frequency, AFP monitoring)
2. Chronological summary of imaging findings and evolution
3. AFP trend analysis and correlation with imaging
4. Suspicious lesions under monitoring (LI-RADS classification if available)
5. Diagnostic or therapeutic interventions performed
6. Current surveillance status and next recommended evaluation

Track both adherence to screening protocols and positive findings.
